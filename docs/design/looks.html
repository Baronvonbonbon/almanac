Three ways almanac could look

Each phone works. Tap Log today, pick a flow, add a symptom and save. The tabs open the calendar and a first look at insights. Every date and log here is example data, and the fertile window is switched on so you can see how it's marked. In the app it's off by default.

Direction
Phones
A

Hearth

The look DESIGN §4 describes: cream, terracotta and a soft serif.

  • CycleA soft ring that fills as the cycle goes on. Period days are solid, the fertile window is dashed, and a dotted arc outside the ring marks when the next period is likely.
  • TypeFraunces at its softest for numbers and headings, DM Sans for everything else.
  • FeelWarm and familiar, exactly as specified. It is also the most common look among wellness apps right now, so it's the least distinctive of the three.

B

Moonpaper

A printed almanac: a dial of days in ink blue and rose.

  • CycleA dial of 29 days, like the day tables in a printed almanac. Every day keeps its place, period days are full dots, the fertile window is open rings, and today is circled.
  • TypeInstrument Serif for the big numbers, Instrument Sans for text, small capital labels.
  • FeelCalm and a little bookish. The dark version, deep ink blue, sits naturally inside the Polkadot app's dark theme.
Day 18Period likely between Sep 25 and Sep 27Instrument Serif · Instrument Sans
C

Pebble

Brighter and rounder: a path of days and the biggest buttons.

  • CycleA path of pebbles in place of a ring, scrolled to today. Striped pebbles are the fertile window, dashed ones the days the next period is likely. This departs from the single ring in DESIGN §3.
  • TypeBricolage Grotesque for headings. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text, a face designed for readers with low vision.
  • FeelFriendlier and more playful, with the largest touch targets and a soft wash of colour behind today.

Same in all three

  • The words: predictions say "likely", and the fertile window is always an estimate.
  • Logging a day takes three taps: Log today, a flow, Save.
  • Meaning never rests on colour alone. Flow is a fill level with a label, predicted days are outlined, and the fertile window has a pattern.
  • Light and dark versions that follow the Polkadot app's theme, not the phone's.
  • In the real app the fonts ship inside almanac, so opening it never contacts a font server. This page borrows them from Google Fonts only to preview them.

How to choose

Reply with a letter, or a mix. "B's dial in A's colours" is a perfectly good answer. The one you pick becomes the app's theme, and the Phase 2 screens are built on it.
